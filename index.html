HELP & CLARIFICATIONS

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU BEGIN?

Essential information regarding training timings, location in Bahiro, and membership enrollment.

What are the gym operating hours? ▼
We operate two daily shifts from Monday to Saturday: Morning from 5:00 AM to 9:00 AM, and Evening from 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays for deep sanitation and recovery.
Where is The Great Champion Gym located in Arrah? ▼
Our gym is located at Singh Saheb Market, New, Bahiro, Arrah, Bihar 802312, India. It offers convenient road access and parking in front of the market complex.
Is the gym suitable for absolute beginners? ▼
Yes! Floor mentors are available during active shifts to demonstrate machine safety, explain basic exercise form, and provide orientation for new members.
How can I enquire about current membership fees? ▼
You can reach us directly via WhatsApp at +91 93041 97481, call us directly, or visit during operating hours (5-9 AM or 4-9 PM) for a walkthrough of the gym floor.
